DAY 02 · TOPIC 04

Discount

Marked Price (MP) is the displayed price of an item. Discount is the reduction offered on the marked price.

Core Formulas

Discount = MP − SP
Discount % = (Discount / MP) × 100
SP = MP × (100 − Discount%)/100

Successive Discounts

Net Discount % = d₁ + d₂ − (d₁ × d₂)/100

This formula gives the equivalent single discount for two successive discounts.

Marked Price & Profit

MP/CP = (100 + Profit%) / (100 − Discount%)

💡 Discount percentage is calculated on MP, while profit/loss percentage is normally calculated on CP.
