Percentage

Percentage means a value expressed out of 100. It is used in income, population, election, price and consumption problems.

Basic Formula

Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

Percentage Change

Increase by x% → New = Original × (100+x)/100
Decrease by x% → New = Original × (100−x)/100

Population Model

New Population = P × (1 ± r/100)

Use + for an increase and − for a decrease.

Election Model

Difference in votes = (Difference in % / 100) × Total valid votes

Price & Consumption

If price increases by x% and expenditure stays constant:
New consumption = Old consumption × 100/(100+x)

💡 Always identify the original quantity before calculating a percentage change.
