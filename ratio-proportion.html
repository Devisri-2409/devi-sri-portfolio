Ratio & Proportion

A ratio compares two quantities of the same kind. A proportion states that two ratios are equal.

Basic Ratio

a : b = a/b

Equivalent ratios can be formed by multiplying or dividing both terms by the same non-zero number.

Finding Unknowns

If x:y = a:b
then x = ka and y = kb

Here k is the common multiplier.

Proportion

a:b = c:d
⇒ a/b = c/d
⇒ ad = bc

Quick Example

If x:y = 5:7 and x+y = 48, then 12 parts = 48, so 1 part = 4. Therefore x = 20 and y = 28.

💡 Keep the order of quantities the same when forming a ratio.
