Profit & Loss

Cost Price (CP) is the price paid to buy an item. Selling Price (SP) is the price at which it is sold.

Core Formulas

Profit = SP − CP
Loss = CP − SP
Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100

Finding SP

SP at p% profit = CP × (100+p)/100
SP at l% loss = CP × (100−l)/100

Successive Profit / Loss

Net % = a + b + (ab/100)
Use + for profit/increase and − for loss/decrease.

Same Selling Price

One item at x% profit and another at x% loss → overall loss = x²/100%

💡 Profit and loss percentages are normally calculated on CP.
